BRANDING

Colsenet

Industry is a demanding world where every minute counts. A single machine failure can have major consequences for your business. Founded by a passionate expert and Excellence Medalist at the Euroskills 2025 competition, Maintenance Colsenet responds quickly and efficiently to industrial emergencies. With a professional, hands-on approach, the company supports businesses in the maintenance and repair of industrial machinery, ensuring reliability, performance and peace of mind.

BRANDING

Deberny Peignot

Final degree project focused on the reopening of the Deberny & Peignot type foundry. The challenge was to bring this historic company back to life in a contemporary context.The project includes the creation of a complete visual identity, a website mockup, motion design pieces, a typographic catalog, and a custom typeface.Following extensive research, I chose to explore the concepts of variability and adaptability, supported by responsive web design, digital composition, and the possibilities offered by new variable typefaces.

BRANDING

Peak pulse

Peak Pulse positions itself as a strong new competitor in the sports retail market, placing accessibility and performance at the core of its values.In a highly competitive industry, Peak Pulse stands out through bold and impactful communication, built around the wave icon and a palette of saturated colors that convey energy, dynamism, and motivation.The visual identity is then deployed across a wide range of digital and physical media, ensuring strong graphic consistency throughout all brand communications.

BRANDING

Dernière pluie

A graphic clothing brand inspired by dystopian aesthetics.This first entrepreneurial-oriented creation project takes into account ecological, economic, graphic, and semantic challenges. Developed as a collaborative project, it introduced me to teamwork and collective creative processes.Dernière Pluie was born from the desire to combine artisanal production with a strong, meaningful message, reflecting a new generation that is conscious of the challenges it faces.The first collection is inspired by the writings of Victor Hugo, a major cultural figure from Besançon.
